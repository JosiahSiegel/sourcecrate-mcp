## Abstract

Deeper neural networks are more difficult to train. We present a residual learning framework to ease the training of networks that are substantially deeper than those used previously.

## Key Contributions

- **Novel Residual Learning Framework**: Introduced a groundbreaking approach to training extremely deep neural networks by reformulating layers to learn residual functions
- **Deep Network Architecture**: Evaluated residual networks up to 152 layers deep, significantly deeper than previous successful architectures
- **Superior Performance**: Achieved 3.57% error on ImageNet test set, winning 1st place in ILSVRC 2015 classification task
- **Cross-Domain Impact**: Demonstrated 28% relative improvement on COCO object detection dataset, showing the general applicability of the approach

## Significance

This paper revolutionized deep learning by solving the degradation problem in very deep networks. The residual learning framework enables training of networks with hundreds of layers, leading to significant improvements in image recognition tasks and becoming a foundational architecture for modern computer vision.

The ResNet architecture has become one of the most influential and widely-used deep learning models, inspiring countless follow-up works and applications across various domains.
